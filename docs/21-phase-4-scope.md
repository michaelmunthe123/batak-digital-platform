# Phase 4 Scope — Community & Contribution

Phase 4 opens a controlled community contribution layer without allowing direct public edits.

## Included

- Invitation-based contributor accounts.
- Contributor activation, login, profile, and optional public profile.
- Contribution proposals for correction, source, new content, relationship, genealogy, language, and media.
- Evidence/source attachments before moderation.
- Sensitive proposal types require at least one evidence source before submission.
- Contribution conversation and evidence-request loop.
- Reviewer/editor/admin moderation queue.
- Accepted / merged / rejected / disputed workflow.
- Revision record when a proposal is accepted or merged.
- Contribution audit trail.
- In-app status notifications.
- Content reports for incorrect information, missing source, cultural sensitivity, privacy, and copyright.
- Invitation and membership suspension controls for admins.
- Public contributor profile and activity aggregates.
- Editorial contribution score as an activity signal only.

## Explicitly excluded

- Direct community mutation of published entities, claims, relationships, genealogy, or sources.
- Open self-registration during the pilot.
- Community voting on cultural truth.
- Public leaderboards that imply cultural authority.
- Self-service personal family trees.
- Automatic merging of crowdsourced genealogy.
- AI or LLM cultural answers/inference.

## Exit criteria

Phase 4 is complete when contributor onboarding, proposal submission, evidence, moderation, reporting, audit, role/RLS controls, and documentation are all represented in the cumulative codebase and pass the Phase 4 verifier.
