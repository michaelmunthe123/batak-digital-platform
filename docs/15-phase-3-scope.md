# Phase 3 Scope — Genealogy & Kinship

Phase 3 turns the cultural knowledge base into a source-aware genealogy and kinship platform without opening community editing yet.

## Included

- Tarombo / genealogy dataset model.
- Public visualizer for explicitly publishable datasets.
- Node/edge provenance and claim status.
- Privacy model for family data.
- Deterministic genealogy path finder.
- Source-aware cultural relationship explorer.
- Partuturan / kinship rule engine.
- Limited pilot rules for Toba and Karo with institutional sources.
- Editorial forms for datasets, nodes, genealogy edges, and kinship rules.
- RLS and database guards.
- Unit and E2E tests.

## Explicitly excluded

- Public user registration and contributor profiles.
- User-owned self-service family trees.
- Community voting/reputation.
- Crowdsourced tarombo publishing.
- AI inference or LLM answers.
- Claiming a relationship solely because two people share or mention a marga.

These exclusions belong to Phase 4 or Phase 5.
