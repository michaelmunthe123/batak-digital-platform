# Phase 5 Scope — AI & Mature Platform

## Goal
Add an AI layer only after Batak Digital already has provenance, editorial workflow, genealogy guardrails, and community governance.

## Deliverables
1. Cultural Assistant with source-grounded answers.
2. Semantic/hybrid search that can be inspected without generation.
3. Personalized learning plans grounded in published sources.
4. AI-assisted editorial review with human-only publishing authority.
5. pgvector document index and repeatable indexing workflow.
6. AI request audit, usage metadata, feedback, rate limiting, and privacy controls.
7. AI transparency documentation and public limitation disclosure.
8. Evaluation-ready deterministic policy tests and Phase 5 verifier.

## Non-goals
- AI-generated genealogy or clan lineage inference.
- AI authority over cultural disputes.
- automatic publication or contribution merging.
- public web browsing from the cultural assistant.
- storing raw prompts by default.
- training/fine-tuning on private living-person genealogy.

## Exit criteria
Phase 5 is complete when the AI surfaces exist, retrieval is source-backed, genealogy is excluded from the public AI index, no AI route can publish knowledge, privacy/security controls are documented, and cumulative Phase 1–5 verification passes.
